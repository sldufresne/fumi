Fumi Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

In short: Fumi does not collect, share or sell your data. Your notes and lists are stored only on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party libraries, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app stores

Fumi stores what you put into it: notes, lists, their items and check marks, and which entries are pinned. This data lives in the app’s storage on your device and is available only to Fumi and its widgets.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t send data to any server: Fumi has no server and never goes online.
  • It doesn’t collect analytics, usage statistics or device information.
  • It doesn’t show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • It doesn’t ask you to sign up or for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • It doesn’t share data with third parties.

iOS features Fumi uses

Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and widgets. Pinned notes and lists appear on the Lock Screen and in widgets, so anyone looking at your phone can see them. Don’t pin anything others shouldn’t see. You can turn off Live Activities on the Lock Screen in iOS Settings: “Fumi” → “Live Activities”.

Dictation, Siri and Shortcuts. If you dictate text, iOS recognizes your speech under Apple’s terms. Fumi only receives the finished text.

Backups. Fumi data is included in your iPhone backup in iCloud or on your computer if you have backups turned on. Backups are managed by Apple and by you; the developer of Fumi has no access to them.

How to delete your data

Delete an entry in the app: swipe it left in the feed and tap “Delete”. To delete all Fumi data, delete the app from your iPhone. Copies in older backups disappear when those backups are overwritten or deleted.

Children

Fumi doesn’t collect data about anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. If Fumi ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so in advance.
